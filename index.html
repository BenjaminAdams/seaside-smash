HOW TO PLAY

WIN POINTS · GAMES · THE MATCH

⏱️ Timing is everything. Hit the ball close to your body for PERFECT strikes — faster, deeper, harder to return.

🎯 Aim with movement. Whatever direction you're pressing as you strike is where the ball goes. Push down for a devilish drop shot, up for a deep drive.

⚡ Charge up. Hold HIT to build power — but an overcharged shot can fly long!

🌊 Serves: toss, then strike at the peak. Land it in the service box. Two faults = point lost.
